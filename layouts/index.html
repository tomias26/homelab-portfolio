Systems Triage & Performance Optimization

Case Study: Resolving Network Switch CPU Spikes & RTSP Stream Degradation

Problem: During security camera stream ingestion, the primary core network switch experienced severe CPU load spikes (reaching 90%+ utilization), causing packet drops and delayed video rendering across local interfaces.

Root-Cause Analysis: Isolated the high CPU utilization to an aggressive High-Bitrate RTSP stream broadcast from a Reolink camera entity flooding un-managed multicast channels across default switch ports.

Resolution: Re-architected traffic paths by isolating all camera interfaces onto a dedicated, non-routable Camera VLAN. Configured IGMP Snooping on Omada managed switches, restricted camera-to-NVR stream paths, and optimized sub-stream parameters in Frigate, stabilizing switch CPU load back to normal baseline levels (<10%).
